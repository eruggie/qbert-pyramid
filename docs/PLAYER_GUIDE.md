# Player guide — Q*Bert Pyramid

The game interface is currently Italian. Nuova partita starts a game;
Informazioni sul gioco shows About/instructions, author Eugenio Ruggieri,
QBERT Rev. 1.1 and GNU GPL v3.0. Impostazioni opens settings, Classifica
shows scores, Presentazione replays the intro, and Esci quits.

Hop through all 28 cells to match the target sample. Permanent, two-step and
reversible rules vary by round. There are four rounds per level and no fixed
last level. Q/Left/keypad7, E/Up/keypad9, Z/Down/keypad1 and C/Right/keypad3
control the four diagonals. P pauses; F11 toggles full screen. Menus use arrows,
Tab, Enter and mouse. Additional movement keys can be assigned in Settings.

Three initial lives; dangerous contacts or falling lose one life. Rescue discs
at the left fourth row and right sixth row are consumed on use and return you
to the apex. Lure the purple chaser toward one to make it fall. The moving green
pickup gives points and five seconds of contact protection/Stasis; falling
remains lethal. Other enemies have different movement and color-changing roles.

Focus loss pauses play. Explicitly resume and release/repress movement keys;
old buffered commands are not replayed. Settings also pause play. Adjust
Master/Effects and mute there. Only event effects are played, no continuous music.

GAME OVER appears after the final death, blinks and lasts up to 30 seconds with
focus, or until a fresh game key/Enter. Results are always shown. A qualifying
positive score accepts exactly three A-Z initials; Backspace corrects them,
Tab/Enter confirms. The ten rows contain initials, points, level and round.
Continue confirms valid qualifying input before returning to the menu.

Records/settings persist under the local Windows profile, not on USB/network
media. Failed writes are reported without blocking play; retry does not duplicate
a score. No in-progress game is saved. See INSTALL_WINDOWS.md for safe updates
and data locations. The previous guide is archived in history/PLAYER_GUIDE_PRE_PHASE7.it.txt.
